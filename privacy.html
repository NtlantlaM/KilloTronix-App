Privacy

Short and plain: what happens when you join the mailing list.

What we collect

Your email address, and your first name if you give it. Nothing else. This site uses no tracking cookies and no ads.

What we use it for

Emails about KilloTronix music: new tracks, release dates, pre-save and release links, and the occasional show or announcement. We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

Where it's kept

Sign-ups are stored with our email service provider, which sends the emails on our behalf. It only processes your details to deliver those emails.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time. We'll act on it promptly, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
