About

The artist

KilloTronix is a producer and audio engineer from Gauteng, South Africa. His catalogue runs through collaborations with Thab De Soul, Native Tribe, Pinochet and Arabic Piano, on records like Woza La, Umoya Wami, Fortunes and the solo cut Magagala, plus remixes of Izinyanda and Nguva.
